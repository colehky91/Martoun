## Lesson 1.1 — Welcome & how to win with this course

**Teaching script:**

Welcome in. Quick truth before anything else: you don't have a follower problem, you
have a *system* problem. At 25k–100k followers you already have more attention than
most small businesses will ever buy — you just haven't built the machine that turns
attention into income. That's what we build here, module by module.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your origin story (60–90 seconds). Who you are, who your partner is,
> what you've done in the creator economy, and the *specific* result that gives you the
> right to teach this. Real numbers, real screenshots. If your proof is client results
> rather than your own account, say that plainly — "we've done this for X creators" is
> credible; vagueness is not.

How to use this course:
1. Go in order. Module 2 depends on Module 1, and so on.
2. Every lesson ends with an Action Step. The course works if you do them; it's a
   $1,000 PDF if you don't.
3. Give it 30 days of honest effort before you judge anything. The 30-Day Action Plan
   at the end schedules all of it for you.

**Action Step:** Write down your #1 income goal for the next 90 days and the single
biggest thing that's stopped you so far. You'll look back at this in Module 7.

## Lesson 1.2 — The money math of a mid-size audience

**Teaching script:**

Let's kill the myth that you need a million followers. Here's the actual math at your
size:

- **Brand deals:** a creator with 50k engaged followers can reasonably charge
  hundreds to a few thousand dollars *per sponsored post* depending on niche and
  engagement. Two deals a month is a real income stream.
- **Your own offer:** if just **1%** of a 50k audience ever buys a $97 product, that's
  ~$48,000. You don't need everyone — you need a tiny, warm slice.
- **High-trust niches** (finance, fitness, B2B, parenting, skincare) monetize far
  better per follower than entertainment/meme accounts. Smaller can literally be worth
  more.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** A real case study at this audience size — you, a client, or a
> documented public example. Show the follower count, the offer, and the revenue.
> One concrete example beats ten hypotheticals. Do NOT invent numbers, and do not
> present your best result as typical.

The lesson: your audience is an asset. This course is about installing the machine
that monetizes it — and the machine has three parts: **Positioning** (Module 1),
**Offer** (Module 2), and **Traffic that converts** (Modules 3–5).

**Action Step:** Calculate your own math: (your follower count) × 1% × ($97). Write
that number somewhere you'll see it. That's the floor, not the ceiling.

## Lesson 1.3 — Positioning: the one-sentence statement

**Teaching script:**

Positioning is the answer to: *why would someone pay YOU instead of scrolling past?*
The formula:

> **I help [WHO] achieve [SPECIFIC OUTCOME] through [YOUR MECHANISM/ANGLE].**

Rules:
- WHO must be narrow enough that a person reads it and thinks "that's literally me."
- OUTCOME must be concrete. "Feel confident" is weak; "book your first paid brand
  deal" is strong.
- MECHANISM is what makes you different — your method, background, or style.

Weak: "I help people get fit."
Strong: "I help new moms rebuild core strength in 15 minutes a day without a gym."

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** 3–5 examples of strong positioning statements *from your niche(s)* —
> ideally real creators the student will recognize. Break down why each one works.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your own positioning statement as the worked example. Show the bad
> first draft and how you sharpened it — the before/after teaches more than the result.

**Action Step (worksheet):**
1. Write 5 versions of your positioning statement.
2. Cross out any word a 12-year-old wouldn't understand.
3. Send the best two to three followers who engage with you most and ask which they'd
   pay for. Keep the winner.

## Lesson 1.4 — The brand audit

**Teaching script:**

Before we build, we take inventory. The student audits four things:

1. **Profile:** Does the bio state the positioning? Does the pinned content prove it?
   Is there exactly one link, and does it go somewhere that captures or sells?
2. **Content (last 30 posts):** Label each post Growth / Trust / Sales (Module 3
   covers these). Most under-monetized creators discover they've posted 30 Growth
   posts and zero Sales posts. That's the diagnosis.
3. **Audience:** Open your analytics. Who actually follows you — age, gender, location?
   Your offer must match who's *there*, not who you imagine.
4. **Assets:** List everything you already have — email list (even 50 people), other
   platforms, past brand contacts, testimonials, skills. Monetization goes faster when
   you start from what exists.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** A screen-recorded walkthrough of YOU auditing a real profile (yours,
> a client's with permission, or a volunteer's). This is the highest-value video in
> Module 1 — students copy what they watch.

**Action Step (worksheet):** Complete the four-part audit. Deliverable: one page —
positioning statement, content mix count (G/T/S), audience snapshot, asset list.
