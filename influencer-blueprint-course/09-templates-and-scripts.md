## 1. Brand outreach — cold email

**Subject:** [Brand] x @[handle] — idea for [product/campaign]

> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm [name], I run @[handle] — [X]k followers of [specific audience] who
> [care about the thing this brand sells]. My audience engagement runs [X]% ,
> and [one proof point: past deal result, or best relevant post stat].
>
> I've been using [product] for [time period] and my audience already asks about
> it — I have an idea for [specific content concept for THIS brand] that would fit
> how you're showing up on [platform].
>
> Media kit here: [link]. Open to a quick call, or happy to work from your brief.
>
> [Name]

Rules: under 120 words, one specific idea, proof over adjectives, link the kit.

**DM variant (3 sentences):** "Hey [Brand] — I'm [name], @[handle], [X]k [niche]
followers at [X]% engagement. My audience already talks about [product category],
and I've got a content idea that fits your current [campaign/season]. Media kit:
[link] — open to your brief."

**Warm variant (brand already follows/engages you):** "Saw you liked the [post] —
that one did [stat]. If you're planning creator content this quarter I'd love to
pitch you one idea. Kit: [link]."

## 2. Brand outreach — follow-ups

**Day 4:** "Hi [Name] — floating this back up. The [concept] idea would land well
with [seasonal/campaign hook]. Kit: [link]."

**Day 10:** "Last nudge from me! If the timing's off this quarter, happy to stay on
your radar for the next one — who's the right person to keep in touch with?"

## 3. Negotiation counters

**Rate too low:** "Thanks [Name] — excited to make this work. That rate covers
content creation only; with [30-day paid usage / the extra deliverable], my rate is
$[X]. If budget is fixed, we could do [smaller package] at $[Y] instead."

**Product-only offer:** "I genuinely like the product, so thank you! I keep gifting
and paid content separate — my content rates start at $[X] and the kit's here:
[link]. If there's no budget this cycle, I'd love to be first in line when there is."

The two counters above are final — use verbatim. The pattern behind both: never a
bare "no"; every counter re-anchors price to a named variable (usage, exclusivity,
deliverable weight) so the brand negotiates scope, not your worth.

## 4. "Comment [WORD]" DM flow (from Module 5.3)

1. **Auto/first reply:** "Here it is! [link] — quick question while you're here:
   [one question about them]?"
2. **After they answer:** respond to THEIR answer specifically (no template can do
   this part — teach students to write one real sentence).
3. **Bridge (if fit):** "Based on that, [offer] is built for exactly this —
   [one-line promise]. Want the link / want me to send the details?"
4. **Follow-up (once, day 2–3):** "Hey [name], closing the loop on [their goal] —
   still thinking about it, or did life happen? Either answer's fine 🙂"

## 5. Launch emails (Module 5.4)

**Email 1 — Open (day 3):** Story of the problem → what you built → what it is,
who it's for, price → CTA.
**Email 2 — Case study (day 4–5):** One person's before/after (real) → what inside
the offer produced it → CTA.
**Email 3 — FAQ (day 7 morning):** The five real objections, answered honestly →
CTA.
**Email 4 — Final hours (day 7 evening):** Short. What's ending (honestly), one
sentence on who this is for, one on who it's NOT for → last CTA.

The four skeletons are the final structure. One rule per email: one story, one
point, one CTA. Plain-spoken subject lines outperform clever ones ("it's open",
"the honest FAQ", "last call — closing tonight").

## 6. Sales-call one-pager (Module 6.4 follow-up)

One page: the promise / who it's for / what's included / timeline / price /
2 testimonials / how to start. Sent after calls for the "ask my partner" case.

Structure final as listed — build once in Canva/Docs, duplicate per offer.

## 7. Media kit skeleton (Module 4.2)

Cover → Audience (screenshots) → Best content + results → Packages & rates →

Skeleton final (5 pages, per Module 4.2). Build in Canva from their media-kit
starters; real analytics screenshots go on pages 2–3.
