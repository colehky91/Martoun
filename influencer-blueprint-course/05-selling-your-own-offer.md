# Module 5 — Selling Your Own Offer

**Student outcome:** a live minimum funnel — content → captured audience (email/DM) →
sales page — plus a launch sequence and a DM sales flow.

---

## Lesson 5.1 — The minimum viable funnel

**Teaching script:**

You don't need a complicated funnel. You need this:

**Content (S posts) → capture point (email list or DM keyword) → sales page → checkout.**

Why capture at all? Because the algorithm shows your posts to a fraction of your
followers, and you don't own the platform. An email list of 500 warm subscribers will
outsell a feed post to 50,000 followers on launch day. Every serious creator business
is built on a captured audience.

**The lead magnet:** a free, instantly-useful thing in exchange for an email — a
checklist, mini-guide, template, or free training. Rule: it should solve ONE narrow
problem in under 15 minutes and naturally lead to the paid offer.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your funnel as the worked example — the actual lead magnet, the
> landing page, the email capture numbers. Screen-record a walkthrough of the whole
> path as a user sees it.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your recommended tool stack with links (landing page builder, email
> platform, checkout — e.g. the ones YOU use), and roughly what it costs per month so
> students can budget.

**Action Step:** Create your lead magnet (one sitting, max 3 hours) and set up the
capture page.

## Lesson 5.2 — The sales page that converts

**Teaching script:**

Sales page structure, top to bottom — this is a fill-in template, not theory:

1. **Headline:** the promise, in the customer's words (use the DM replies from
   Module 2 validation).
2. **The problem:** describe their situation better than they can. "You post every
   day, the views come, and the bank account doesn't move."
3. **The shift:** why the usual advice fails and what your mechanism does differently.
4. **What's inside:** deliverables as outcomes ("Module 4: send your first 20 brand
   pitches") not features ("14 videos").
5. **Proof:** testimonials, results, screenshots. Real ones only — and never present
   an outlier result as typical (Module 8 covers why this is a legal issue, not just
   an ethical one).
6. **Price + guarantee/terms:** state it plainly. Whatever refund policy you choose,
   honor it exactly as written.
7. **FAQ:** the 5 objections from your DMs.
8. **Final CTA.**

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your own sales page, annotated section by section — "here's why this
> headline, here's the DM this testimonial came from." An annotated real page teaches
> 10x better than the template alone.

**Action Step (worksheet):** Draft each of the 8 sections in a doc. Ship an ugly v1 —
copy quality matters more than design.

## Lesson 5.3 — Selling in the DMs (without being spammy)

**Teaching script:**

At this audience size, DMs are your highest-converting channel. The flow, triggered by
"comment [WORD]" CTAs or story replies:

1. **Deliver first:** send the promised link/resource immediately. No games.
2. **Open a real conversation:** one question about THEM ("what are you working on /
   what's your follower count / what's the goal?"). Then listen.
3. **Diagnose:** their answer tells you if your offer fits. If it doesn't, say so and
   help anyway — that person refers others.
4. **Bridge:** "based on what you said, [offer] would fit because [their words]. Want
   the link / want to hop on a quick call?" (Call for high-ticket → Module 6.)
5. **Follow up once** after 2–3 days if they went quiet. Once. Politely. Then let it go.

Non-negotiables: never cold-DM strangers with a pitch, never copy-paste walls of text,
never argue with a no. The DM flow only works on people who raised their hand.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** 2–3 real (anonymized) DM conversations that ended in a sale — the
> actual messages. Annotate the turning points. This is consistently the lesson
> students say was worth the course price.

**Action Step:** Run one "comment [WORD]" post this week and work every reply through
the 5-step flow. Log outcomes.

## Lesson 5.4 — The launch sequence

**Teaching script:**

When the offer goes live (or reopens), run a 7-day sequence instead of one lonely
announcement post:

- **Days 1–2 (seed):** Trust content about the problem. Tease that something's coming.
- **Day 3 (open):** the direct-ask post + email #1 (story + what it is).
- **Days 4–5 (proof + objections):** result post, FAQ post; email #2 (case study).
- **Day 6 (urgency — honest only):** founding price ends / bonus expires / doors
  close. Fake countdowns destroy trust and can violate consumer-protection rules —
  only use urgency that's true.
- **Day 7 (close):** last-call post + emails #3 and #4 (morning FAQ, evening final
  hours). Half your sales will come on the last day. That's normal — don't panic on
  day 5.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your real launch calendar and results, day by day — including the
> flat middle days, so students don't quit on day 5. All email copy skeletons are in
> the swipe file; drop in your actual launch emails as examples.

**Action Step (worksheet):** Fill in the 7-day calendar: each day's post(s), story
sequence, and email, with dates.
