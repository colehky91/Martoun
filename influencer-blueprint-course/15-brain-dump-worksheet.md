# Brain-Dump Worksheet — Answer These, I'll Do the Rest

The fastest way to build this course: you and Nick answer the questions below in
plain, messy language — voice-memo transcripts are perfect, typos welcome. Don't
write "course content," just tell the truth like you're talking to me on a call.
When it's filled in, hand it back and I'll turn the answers into polished lesson
scripts, sales copy, and the case-study videos' outlines.

Split the work: **you** answer the strategy/system questions, **Nick** answers the
artist-experience ones (marked 🎤). Disagree with each other where you do — the
disagreements are usually the best content.

---

## A. The origin (feeds the case study, file 12)

1. ✏️ How did this start? Whose idea was TikTok? What was the exact conversation?
2. ✏️ 🎤 Nick — what did you think of the plan at the beginning, honestly?
3. ✏️ What were the numbers on day one? (followers, listeners, anything)
4. ✏️ What was the very first video you posted, and what happened?
5. ✏️ How long until the first real hit? How many posts in? What almost made you quit?

## B. The breakthrough (feeds files 12–13)

6. ✏️ Describe the first video that popped: concept, hook, sound, why do YOU think it
   worked?
7. ✏️ What did you do in the 48 hours after it popped?
8. ✏️ When did it become repeatable? What was the pattern you spotted?
9. ✏️ Of your top 10 videos ever — list them and what they have in common.
10. ✏️ Your 3 biggest flops that you expected to win — what did each teach you?

## C. The system (feeds file 13 — go deep here, this is the product)

11. ✏️ Walk me through a week: how many posts, filmed when, posted when, edited how?
12. ✏️ Your hook rules — what has to happen in the first 3 seconds?
13. ✏️ How do you pick sounds? When do you ride a trend vs. push Nick's own music?
14. ✏️ Which analytics do you check after posting, and what do they change?
15. ✏️ 🎤 Nick — what does the on-camera side actually take? Energy, takes per video,
    what felt cringe at first, what you'd tell an artist starting today?
16. ✏️ What tools do you use? (editing, captions, scheduling — name them)
17. ✏️ If a client could only follow FIVE rules from your system, what are they?

## D. The conversion (feeds file 14)

18. ✏️ How do you turn a viewer into a follower? What actually moves that number?
19. ✏️ How did you move TikTok viewers to Spotify? Exact mechanics — bio link, teasers,
    release timing, pre-saves, whatever you did.
20. ✏️ When a video went viral, how fast and how much did Spotify move?
21. ✏️ 🎤 Nick — a fan moment that made this feel real (a DM, a cover, a show moment)?
22. ✏️ What's the engagement rate, how do you calculate it, where are the screenshots?

## E. The opinions (feeds the marketing — spice section)

23. ✏️ What does everyone in the industry believe about growth that you now know is
    wrong?
24. ✏️ Why organic over ads — make the argument like you're arguing with a label exec.
25. ✏️ What separates the artists/creators who blow up from the equally-talented ones
    who don't?
26. ✏️ What would you NEVER do again?

## F. The receipts (feeds everything)

27. ✏️ Drop links/screenshots in a shared folder and paste the link here: ___
    (TikTok analytics, top videos, Spotify for Artists, follower graphs, comment
    sections, anything with dates visible.)
