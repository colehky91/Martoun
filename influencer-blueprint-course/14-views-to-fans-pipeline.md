# Views → Followers → Streams → Fans: The Conversion Pipeline

100M views is the headline, but the deeper flex is the conversion: views became
followers, followers became 60M streams, and streams became a real fanbase with
serious engagement. Most creators get views and keep nothing. This file extracts how
you converted at each stage — it becomes its own module, and it's what separates your
course from every generic "how to go viral" product.

---

## Stage 1 — Views → Followers

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** What makes a viewer follow instead of scroll on? What did you do in
> Nick's videos/profile that converted viewers — recurring series they didn't want to
> miss? A cliffhanger structure? Consistent identity per video? Your follower-per-view
> rate on different formats (section 3 of the growth-system file) plugs in here.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Anything you deliberately did in captions/comments/replies to turn a
> viral video's viewers into followers during the spike.

## Stage 2 — Followers → Streams (the platform jump)

The hardest jump: TikTok wants to keep people on TikTok. You moved millions to Spotify.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Exactly how. Song-teaser strategy (tease on TikTok, full version on
> Spotify)? Pre-save campaigns? Link-in-bio setup? Release timing around viral moments?
> "Out now" follow-up videos? What share of a viral spike showed up in Spotify numbers,
> and how fast? For non-music students, generalize the principle: how to move audience
> from the discovery platform to the platform where you get paid / own the relationship.

## Stage 3 — Streams → Fans (audience becomes fanbase)

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** What turned listeners into an engaged fanbase — the "wicked
> engagement rate"? Community behaviors you encouraged (comment culture, replying,
> duets/stitches of Nick's songs, live streams, behind-the-scenes intimacy)? What did
> Nick do that made fans feel like insiders early?

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Compute and state the engagement rate you brag about, and show the
> receipts (real comment sections, share counts). Define how you measure it so the
> claim is defensible.

## Stage 4 — Fans → Money (closing the loop for students)

The course's monetization modules (4–6) handle the mechanics — this section is where
you connect YOUR pipeline to them.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** What did the fanbase make possible for Nick — streaming revenue,
> shows, merch, brand interest, label leverage? Even if monetization came later or is
> still developing, tell it straight; the honest sequencing ("attention first, money
> second, and here's when to flip the switch") IS the teaching.
