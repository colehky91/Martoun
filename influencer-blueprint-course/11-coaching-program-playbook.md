# INTERNAL — The $10k Coaching Tier Playbook

**Do not ship this file to students.** This is your delivery manual for the
"Blueprint + Live Coaching" tier.

## What the $10k buyer is actually paying for

Not more information — they have the course. They're paying for: speed (you make the
decisions with them), accountability (they actually do the work), access (their
specific situation reviewed by you), and outcomes. Structure the program so those four
things are unmistakable, and the price defends itself. If the coaching tier is just
"the course + a group chat," refund requests will be right.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Define your capacity honestly: how many coaching clients can the two
> of you serve well at once? Start smaller than you think (5–8). Oversold coaching is
> how reputations die at exactly the moment yours is being built.

## Program structure — 12 weeks

**Format:** weekly 45–60 min 1:1 (or small-pod) call + async access (Slack/WhatsApp,
one guaranteed response per business day) + deliverable reviews between calls.

| Week | Focus | Deliverable you review |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Deep-dive audit + 90-day plan | Their brand audit, done WITH them live |
| 2 | Positioning locked | Final statement + updated profile |
| 3 | Offer design | Offer one-pager + price |
| 4 | Validation sprint | DM/presell results — go/revise decision |
| 5 | Content engine install | First batch week, reviewed post-by-post |
| 6 | Media kit + rate card | You mark up their kit before it ships |
| 7 | Outreach sprint | First 20 pitches — you edit the first 5 live |
| 8 | Funnel build | Lead magnet + capture page review |
| 9 | Sales page teardown | You rewrite their weakest sections live |
| 10 | Launch planning | 7-day calendar + all email drafts reviewed |
| 11 | LAUNCH WEEK | Daily async support; you're in their corner |
| 12 | Retro + next quarter | Numbers review + scaling-lever decision |

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Adjust the arc to your real availability and decide 1:1 vs. pods of
> 3–4 (pods scale better and clients like the peer group — but only mix similar
> niches/levels).

## Onboarding (week 0)

- Intake form: analytics screenshots, income to date, offer status, goal, hours/week
  available, biggest blocker.
- Contract + payment (full or 2–3 installments; get the agreement lawyer-reviewed
  once — Module 8 logic applies to you too).
- Welcome call (15 min): expectations, calendar, communication rules.
- **Set the results expectation in writing:** you guarantee the process and your
  attention, never a revenue number. Do not sell $10k coaching with income promises —
  it's the fastest route to disputes, chargebacks, and regulator attention.

## Weekly call structure (keep every call to this)

1. Wins since last call (5 min)
2. This week's deliverable review — screen share, concrete edits (25 min)
3. One decision to make together (10 min)
4. Next week's commitment, stated by THEM, written down (5 min)

## Between-call rules

- They submit deliverables 24h before calls; no deliverable = call becomes a working
  session, not a chat.
- Async questions answered within one business day. Voice notes are fine and faster.
- You keep a running doc per client: goals, decisions, commitments, numbers. This doc
  is also your testimonial evidence at week 12 (ask permission before using anything).

## Retention & results hygiene

- Week 6 mid-point check: "is this worth it so far?" asked directly. Catch drift early.
- Week 12 exits: retro, documented results, testimonial ask, then offer the
  continuation (monthly retainer at a lower rate, or alumni pod).
- Track every client's before/after numbers with permission — this becomes the
  (honest, documented) proof that sells the next cohort.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your continuation offer and price. Also decide your refund/pause
> policy for coaching BEFORE the first client asks — mid-program refund questions are
> much easier with a written policy.

## Selling this tier (for your sales calls)

Qualify hard: coaching buyers should already have ≥25k followers, a niche with buyer
intent, and hours to invest weekly. The Module 6 call framework applies verbatim —
you're the doctor. Turning down a bad-fit $10k is painful once and profitable forever;
a failed flagship client at your scale costs more than the fee.
