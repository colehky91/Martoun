# Module 3 — The Content Engine

**Student outcome:** a weekly content system with the right mix of growth, trust, and
sales content — batched so it takes one working session per week.

---

## Lesson 3.1 — The three jobs of content

**Teaching script:**

Every post has exactly one job. Under-monetized creators post 100% job-one content:

- **Growth (G):** made to reach strangers. Trends, broad hooks, shareable. Grows the
  audience, sells nothing.
- **Trust (T):** made for existing followers. Your story, behind-the-scenes, results,
  opinions, teaching depth. Turns followers into fans.
- **Sales (S):** made to move people to action. Talks about the offer, shows proof,
  has a clear CTA.

**The working mix at this stage: roughly 3 Growth / 3 Trust / 1–2 Sales per week.**
Sales content underperforms on reach — that is fine and expected. It's not there to go
viral; it's there to convert the audience the G and T posts built. Judge S posts on
DMs, clicks, and sales. Never on views.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Three real examples from your own account (or clients') — one G, one
> T, one S post — with the actual results of each. Show the S post's low views next to
> the money it made. That contrast is the whole lesson.

**Action Step:** Re-label your last 30 posts G/T/S (you started this in the Module 1
audit). Write your target weekly mix.

## Lesson 3.2 — Sales content formats that don't kill your account

**Teaching script:**

Six S-post formats to rotate — none of them feel like ads:

1. **Result/proof post:** a win (yours or a customer's), the story of how, soft CTA.
2. **Myth-buster:** "everyone tells you X, here's what actually works" → your
   mechanism → CTA.
3. **Process peek:** show a piece of the offer being made or used. Curiosity does the
   selling.
4. **FAQ post:** answer the #1 objection you hear in DMs, publicly.
5. **Story sequence (IG stories / TikTok series):** problem → agitation → solution →
   CTA across 4–6 frames. Stories can be far more direct than feed posts.
6. **Direct ask:** once in a while, just say it plainly: what it is, who it's for,
   what it costs, where to get it. Honesty converts.

**CTA rules:** one CTA per post. "Comment [WORD] and I'll DM you the link" outperforms
"link in bio" at this audience size — it creates a DM conversation (Module 5 shows how
to run it).

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** One scripted example of each of the six formats, written for a
> sample niche — hooks and all — so students can copy the skeleton. Use your real
> posts wherever you have them.

**Action Step:** Write two S posts this week using formats 1 and 4. Post one.

## Lesson 3.3 — The batching system

**Teaching script:**

The engine only works if it's sustainable. The one-session weekly system:

1. **Idea bank (15 min):** every DM question, comment, and objection goes into a
   running list. You never start from a blank page.
2. **Script (45 min):** write hooks first — 5 hooks, pick 2. Then bullet the body.
   Hook formulas: number+outcome ("3 things that…"), negative ("stop doing…"),
   direct-callout ("if you have 25k followers and no income…").
3. **Batch record (60–90 min):** all week's videos in one sitting, same setup.
4. **Schedule (15 min):** load the week into a scheduler. Done.

> ✏️ **FILL IN:** Your actual tool stack (scheduler, editing app, idea-bank tool) with
> a 2–3 minute screen demo of your real workflow. Name specific tools you actually use.

> ✏️ **FILL IN (optional but powerful):** A filmed "batch day" — sped-up footage of
> you scripting and recording a week of content in one session.

**Action Step:** Block one 3-hour session this week and produce 7 days of content.
The 30-Day Plan assumes this happens weekly.
