## The transformation (say this on your sales page and in Module 1)

**Before:** You have 25k–100k followers, decent engagement, maybe the occasional free
product in the mail — and no reliable income from any of it.

**After:** You know exactly what you sell, who buys it, and what to post to sell it.
You have a media kit, a rate card, an outreach system for brand deals, and at least one
offer of your own — with a repeatable path to your first (or next) $10k.

## Module summaries

**Module 1 — Welcome & Foundations.** Orientation, how to use the course, the money
math of a mid-size audience. Then the two foundation exercises: the niche/positioning
statement and the brand audit. Nobody skips to tactics until these are done.

**Module 2 — Your Offer.** The four monetization paths (brand deals, digital products,
services/coaching, affiliate). How to pick ONE based on niche, audience trust, and time.
Offer design: promise, deliverable, price. Validation before you build.

**Module 3 — The Content Engine.** Why growth content and sales content are different
jobs. The weekly content mix, the conversion post formats, CTAs that don't kill reach,
and a batching system so this takes hours, not days.

**Module 4 — Brand Deals.** The channel that works at 25k–100k *today*. Media kit,
rate math (and why creators undercharge 3–5x), building a target list, cold outreach,
inbound conversion, negotiation, usage rights, and red flags.

**Module 5 — Selling Your Own Offer.** Owning the sale: landing page, lead magnet,
email/DM capture, the launch sequence, and selling in DMs without being spammy.

**Module 6 — Sales Calls & Closing.** For offers above ~$300. The discovery-call
framework, handling "I can't afford it", following up, and when NOT to close someone.

**Module 7 — Operations & Scaling.** The boring stuff that makes it durable: tool
stack, finance rhythm, first hires (editor, VA), and the three scaling levers.

**Module 8 — Legal & Money Basics.** #ad disclosure rules, reading a brand contract,
usage rights and exclusivity, when to form an entity, and setting aside tax money.
(Educational only — tell students to get a real lawyer/accountant for their situation.)
